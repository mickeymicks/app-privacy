App Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

This policy describes how apps developed by Michael Sesbreno handle your information.

  • DigiCaliper
  • GpioMate
  • BoltMate
  • CircuitMate

DigiCaliper

DigiCaliper calculates measurements on your device. It does not send your measurements to the developer or to Google. It does not require an account. The app saves manual calibration and appearance preferences in app storage. You can reset manual calibration in the app or remove the stored preferences by clearing the app's data or deleting the app and its data.

On iOS, DigiCaliper uses Google AdMob to show banner ads on the Help screen. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share information such as your IP address and approximate location, device identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, and diagnostic and performance data. Google may use this information to deliver and measure ads, perform analytics, prevent fraud, and improve its services.

DigiCaliper uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage ad consent where applicable. When Ad privacy choices appears on the Help screen, you can use it to review or change your choices. See the Google Privacy Policy for Google's data handling and retention practices and My Ad Center for Google ad settings.

GpioMate

GpioMate is in development. Its planned offline features include importing a board image you select, creating pin assignments and annotations, and saving projects on your device. The planned design does not require an account or cloud storage for those features. If you choose to export or share a diagram, the selected destination receives the exported content and handles it under its own privacy practices.

BoltMate

BoltMate runs its fastener lookups and calculations on your device without an account. It saves favourites, notes, shopping links and their labels, and appearance preferences in app storage. You can remove saved items, links, or notes in the app or remove stored app data through your device.

If you open a reference or shopping link, BoltMate hands the selected address to an external app such as your browser. The site you visit may receive standard connection information and apply its own privacy policy.

CircuitMate

CircuitMate is in development. Its calculators and visualisations are designed to process values you enter, such as voltage, resistance, and current, on your device. The planned core features do not require an account or an internet connection.
